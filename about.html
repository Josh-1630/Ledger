Ledger

A private money manager for your phone

Ledger tracks what you earn and spend, keeps you inside the budgets you set, and helps you save toward goals. It runs in your phone's browser and installs to your home screen like a normal app.

Open Ledger Privacy policy

What it does

Expenses and income

Record spending, income and transfers with categories, subcategories, notes and receipt photos. See them by day, on a calendar or by month.

Budgets with alerts

Give each category a monthly limit. Ledger warns you at 80% and asks before an expense takes you over. Unspent budget can roll into next month.

Goals and insights

Save toward goals with a weekly target, and see month-to-month changes, no-spend days and a six-month trend.

Works offline

After the first visit Ledger opens and saves without internet. An optional PIN locks it.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on your own device. Ledger has no server and no user accounts of its own, and it does not use analytics or advertising.

If you choose Sign in with Google, Ledger backs up your data and receipt photos to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so you can restore them on a new phone. Ledger can only see that one folder, not your other Drive files, and it uses your email address only to show which account the backup is in. The privacy policy explains this in full.

Install it

  • Android: open Ledger in Chrome, then tap Install app (or menu, then Install app).
  • iPhone: open Ledger in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
